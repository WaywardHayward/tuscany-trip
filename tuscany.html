A slow green fortnight in Tuscany

Same hidden-garden feeling as Saint-Paul, all the way there by train, and not a hangover in sight.

~12 daysEdinburgh to the hills by railOne garden baseGardens & hill towns

The plan is simple: find a pool tucked into a garden, sink into it, and let Tuscany come to us at walking pace.

We loved the south of France for the perched stone villages, the art and the green calm. Tuscany has all of that with a different accent: a farmhouse-and-pool to come home to, two of the great garden villas down the road, Florence for the art, and a string of hill towns that feel like Saint-Paul's Italian cousins. Vegetarian heaven, brilliant without a drink, and reachable on lovely long trains the whole way.

Good to know

The practical bits

Getting there on rails

Edinburgh → London → Eurostar to Paris → high-speed down through Turin → Florence → the hills. One thing to dodge: the Paris–Milan line fully closes for works from 14 Sept to 13 Oct 2026, so travel before mid-September or after mid-October.

Eurostar SNCF Connect Seat61 route guide

The car question

Lucca, Florence and Pisa are dead easy by train. The hidden base, the villa gardens and the deep hill towns really want a car, though. Cleanest answer: keep the long haul on rails and hire a small car just for the Tuscan leg, dropping it before the train home.

Eating well, meat-free

Tuscany is a vegetarian's happy place. Reliable joys to look for:

  • Ribollita and pappa al pomodoro
  • Pici with cacio e pepe or garlic
  • Panzanella, white beans in good oil
  • Pecorino, fresh focaccia, proper tomatoes

Lovely without a drink

Italy is built for this. The evening ritual works perfectly dry: order a Crodino, a Sanbittèr or anything marked analcolico and you get the bitter-orange aperitivo and the little snacks, minus the booze. Coffee culture, gelato and the thermal pool at Bagno Vignoni do the rest.

So. Shall we?

A garden to disappear into, a few perfect days out, and the whole thing rolling past a train window. Exactly what a threadbare pair ordered.
