This is the one where we don't plan a thing: fall asleep on a ferry, wake up to white sand and water the colour of a swimming pool, and let a great resort do the rest.

You asked for Ikos-level luxury without ever boarding a plane — so here it is. Forte Village, on Sardinia's soft southern coast, is one of Europe's legendary beach resorts: a gated village of five-star hotels strung along a private white-sand beach, with a thalasso spa, a dozen-plus restaurants, gardens, pools and the bluest sea on the island. Getting there is half the romance — Eurostar to Paris, a fast run south through France and Italy, and then a cabin on an overnight ferry that drops you 40 minutes from the sand. Do nothing for a week, or hop out to Roman ruins, hilltop Cagliari and the turquoise coves whenever the mood takes you.

The plan

Forte Village, Santa Margherita di Pula

A gated resort village on a private white-sand beach 40 minutes from Cagliari, with a famous thalasso spa, more than twenty restaurants, pools, gardens and sports. You never have to leave — but Roman ruins and turquoise coves are a short hop when you want them.

Here's the thing: Forte Village is one resort, but inside it sits a cluster of eight separate hotels at different grades — all sharing the same beach, spa, pools and restaurants. You don't book "Forte Village"; you pick which hotel inside it you sleep in. That single choice sets your price and your vibe:

The practical bits

Getting there — rail & sea

The whole point: no planes. Trains to the Mediterranean, then a cabin overnight.

  • Edinburgh → London → Paris · LNER ~4h20 + Eurostar ~2h20 · overnight in Paris
  • Paris → the Italian coast · TGV south to the Riviera and on to a ferry port (a night en route)
  • Civitavecchia → Cagliari · overnight ferry ~13h with a cabin, landing ~40 min from the resort · or Genoa → Porto Torres for the north

The resort & getting around

Forte Village handles transfers from the port (or from Cagliari station). Inside, everything's a short walk or a buggy ride — beach, spa, pools and restaurants all on site. For day trips, take the resort's own excursions or hire a car for the day: Nora ~10 min, Chia ~30 min, Cagliari ~40 min.

Eating well, meat-free

Sardinia's island table, plus the resort's many restaurants:

  • Culurgiones — potato-and-mint pasta parcels
  • Fregola with tomato, artichokes, stuffed vegetables
  • Pane carasau, pecorino, sun-warm tomatoes and figs
  • Seadas with honey, and gelato in Pula

The splurge, and when

This is the indulgent one — Forte Village is a genuine luxury benchmark, and in peak it sits at the top of the budget. Aim for June or September: the prices and crowds ease, the sea is still bath-warm, and the southern beaches are at their most ridiculous shade of blue.

Switch off completely?

A cabin across the sea, a private beach, a thalasso spa and water like glass — all of it without ever seeing an airport. Back to the other options whenever.
