The Côte d'Azur with a Spanish accent: warm and Mediterranean, minus the southern furnace.

Barcelona is the smart way to do warm Spain in summer: high-20s with a sea breeze, gardens and art in spades, and a direct train from Paris. A few days of Gaudí, markets and the Gothic Quarter, then up to Girona and the Costa Brava for whitewashed artist villages, medieval stone towns, clifftop gardens and Dalí. Coast like the south of France, perched stone like Saint-Paul, and vegetarian heaven.

Where we'd stay

Two bases: city, then coast

Barcelona first for the art and the buzz, then Girona and the Empordà for the calm.

The plan

Barcelona first, the coast second

Three nights in Barcelona puts Gaudí, the Boqueria and the Gothic Quarter on your doorstep. Then four nights based in Girona (or a Costa Brava town like Begur), with the medieval villages, Cadaqués and the coastal gardens all an easy day out.

Good to know

The practical bits

Getting there on rails

The best rail story of the lot — one direct high-speed train does the hard part:

  • Edinburgh → London → Paris · LNER ~4h10 + Eurostar ~2h20 · overnight Paris
  • Paris → Barcelona · direct double-deck TGV ~6h40, calling at Figueres & Girona
  • Barcelona → Girona · AVE/Avant ~38 min for the coastal leg

A night in Paris and you're on La Rambla the next afternoon.

Getting around

Barcelona's metro is excellent, and Girona and Figueres sit on the high-speed line. The Costa Brava coast and the Empordà villages (Cadaqués, Besalú, Pals) are reached by local bus from Girona and Figueres. Montserrat is train plus rack railway from the city.

Eating well, meat-free

Catalan cooking loves its veg:

  • Pa amb tomàquet (tomato-rubbed bread) with everything
  • Escalivada, grilled veg, wild mushrooms
  • Patatas bravas, padrón peppers, tortilla
  • The Boqueria for fruit, juices and grazing

Long Mediterranean evenings

Order an orxata (horchata), a granissat or a cafè amb gel and settle in. The markets, the late golden light and the long seafront evenings do the rest.

Worth a flutter?

Gaudí in the morning, a medieval village at lunch, a clifftop garden in the afternoon, and the sea the whole way. Back to the other options whenever.
