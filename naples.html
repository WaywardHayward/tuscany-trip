A change of pace from Tuscany: two thousand years of history, a volcano, and a bay that's all blue and lemon.

Naples is loud, chaotic and gloriously alive, the home of pizza and the keeper of everything dug out of Pompeii. We'd give it two nights, then drop down to Sorrento for the calm: cliff-top lemon groves, the ruins a short train away, Capri across the water, and gardens with views that go forever. History and art like Rome, perched stone like Saint-Paul, vegetarian heaven, and the best pizza on earth.

Where we'd stay

Two bases: buzzy, then calm

A couple of nights in the thick of Naples, then down to Sorrento to breathe out.

The plan

Naples first, Sorrento second

Two nights in Naples' centro storico puts the museums, the Veiled Christ and the pizza on your doorstep. Then three nights in Sorrento, a calmer cliff-top base with ferries to Capri and the little Pompeii train, so the ruins and islands are easy days out.

Good to know

The practical bits

Getting there on rails

Naples is the deep south, so it's the best part of two days each way — gorgeous if you lean in, with an overnight in Milan:

  • Edinburgh → London → Paris · LNER ~4h10 + Eurostar ~2h20
  • Paris → Milan · Frecciarossa ~7h · overnight in Milan
  • Milan → Naples · Frecciarossa ~4h40, direct and frequent

If time's tight, this is the one trip where a three-hour flight genuinely earns its keep.

Around the bay

The Circumvesuviana threads Naples → Herculaneum → Pompeii Scavi (~35 min) → Sorrento (~65 min, every 30 min) — cheap and frequent if a bit rough; the faster Campania Express is worth the few extra euros. Hydrofoils reach Capri in ~20–25 min from Sorrento. Note: the museums and Caserta shut on Tuesdays.

Eating well, meat-free

You're in pizza's birthplace, so this is no hardship:

  • Pizza margherita and marinara, done properly
  • Parmigiana di melanzane (aubergine)
  • Friarielli, pasta e patate, fried street snacks
  • Sfogliatella and babà to finish

Coffee, granita & the passeggiata

Coffee is a religion here, so an espresso at the bar is half the fun. Lemon granita and gelato are everywhere, and the long lungomare passeggiata is the best part of the day.
