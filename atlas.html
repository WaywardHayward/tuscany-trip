The slow road to North Africa: eight days of trains and one ferry, bookending a five-star Berber kasbah with Toubkal looming over the infinity pool.

This one's a happy lark — the journey is the adventure. Eurostar to Paris, the long lovely run down through France into Spain, a night in Algeciras within sight of Africa, then the ferry across the Strait of Gibraltar with two continents shaking hands off the bow. From Tangier the high-speed line whisks you to Marrakech, and a car carries you up into the High Atlas to Kasbah Tamadot — Richard Branson's mountain hideaway above Asni, all Berber-chic suites, heated pools, a hammam, and the snow-streaked roof of North Africa filling the windows. Between long lazy resort days, the mountains and the medina come to you: Imlil's walnut groves and mule paths, the souks and gardens of Marrakech, the waterfalls of the Ourika, and the great ruined mosque at Tin Mal. Then you do it all in reverse and arrive home with the smuggest, most low-carbon tan in Edinburgh. Tagines at altitude, no boarding passes, zero shoes removed for security.

Good to know

The practical bits

Getting there on rails (& one ferry)

Four unhurried days down, all by train bar the crossing:

  • Edinburgh → London → Paris · LNER ~4h20 + Eurostar ~2h20 · overnight in Paris
  • Paris → Barcelona · direct TGV ~6h40 · overnight
  • Barcelona → Madrid → Algeciras · AVE + Andalucía line · overnight by the strait
  • Algeciras → Tangier · ferry ~1h–1h30, then ONCF Al Boraq & connection on to Marrakech
  • Marrakech → Kasbah Tamadot · ~1h private transfer up into the Atlas

Around the High Atlas

The kasbah is the hub, and it arranges the mountain roads — Imlil, the Ourika, Tin Mal and Marrakech are all easy guided day-trips by car. Marrakech itself is a walkable medina once you're down there. Essaouira and the coast come by coach. The only non-rail leg of the whole holiday is that single ferry — and the short mountain transfers the resort drives for you.

Eating well, meat-free

Morocco is a joy for vegetarians — the spice does the heavy lifting:

  • Vegetable tagines with prunes, apricots and almonds; seven-veg couscous on Fridays
  • Zaalouk and taktouka — smoky aubergine and pepper salads — with warm khobz
  • Harira (ask for the meat-free pot), bissara, and briouats of cheese and herbs
  • Mint tea, fresh orange juice, oranges with cinnamon, and almond pastries

Heat, altitude & the resort

Marrakech bakes in high summer — often nudging 40°C — which is exactly why you're based up in the mountains, where the kasbah sits in the high-20s with cool nights. Save the medina and the valleys for mornings and late afternoons, keep the fierce middle of the day for the pool or the hammam, and carry water and sun cover on any walk. The altitude is gentle at the resort; only a Toubkal attempt needs real acclimatising.
