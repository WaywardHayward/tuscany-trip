You already love the Hôtel Welcome at Villefranche — so let's make a whole trip of it: one perfect bay to come home to, and the most beautiful coast in France out the door.

The Côte d'Azur is the original glamour coast, and the loveliest thing about it is that you barely need a car. Base yourself on the Cap — buttercup-yellow Villefranche-sur-Mer, or the green peninsula of Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat next door — and the little coastal train threads the whole show together: the Rothschild gardens, the eagle's-nest village of Èze, the harbour and casino of Monaco, lemon-coloured Menton on the Italian line, and old Nice with its markets and pebble beach. Deep-blue water for swimming, a coastal path for walking, and somewhere wonderful for dinner every single night.

Good to know

The practical bits

Getting there on rails

All the way by train, the easy way south:

  • Edinburgh → London → Paris · LNER ~4h20 + Eurostar ~2h20 · overnight in Paris
  • Paris → Nice · direct TGV ~5h50 from Gare de Lyon, or a little quicker via Marseille
  • Nice → the Cap · coastal TER to Villefranche ~6 min / Beaulieu (for Cap-Ferrat) ~9 min

The coastal train

This is the magic carpet of the trip — the TER line hugs the shore and runs every 20–30 minutes. From the base: Nice ~6 min, Èze-sur-Mer ~13 min, Monaco ~20 min, Menton ~35 min, Antibes ~35 min. Buses (the 15/80) climb to hilltop Èze village and round Cap-Ferrat. No car needed anywhere.

Eating well, meat-free

The sunny Niçois and Ligurian table:

  • Socca, the chickpea pancake, hot from the griddle
  • Pan-bagnat and salade niçoise (hold the anchovy)
  • Ratatouille, courgette-flower beignets, pissaladière
  • Menton lemon tart, and gelato on every corner

Heat, sea & when to go

July and August are hot, busy and dear on this coast. June or September are the sweet spot — warm sea, lighter crowds, kinder prices. Do the gardens and the Èze climb in the morning, save the middle of the day for a shaded café or a swim off the rocks, and let the evenings be for the harbour.
